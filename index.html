VoltBench

Build an Arduino circuit, write the C++ sketch, upload it and watch it run. Then assemble a line follower robot and program it around a track.

Learn the parts

Pick a topic. Each one opens a live demo that starts on its own, so you can see how sensors, motors, line following and maze solving work. Then jump straight into the matching lab.

Breadboard workbench

1. Pick a part, then click a breadboard hole to place it. 2. Pick a wire colour, click one point, then another to connect them. Click a wire to remove it. 3. Power the Arduino from the battery (battery + to VIN, battery - to GND) and feed the breadboard rails from the 5V and GND pins. 4. Upload your sketch and run it.

      Test conditions

      Write your sketch below, press Upload, then Run. If the car misbehaves, go back and fix the wiring or the design, then upload the car again.

      Track obstacles

      Add, move or remove obstacles, then press Reset and Run. The car has to be built to cope: tyres, ballast and stance all change the result.

      

      

      VoltBench runs your sketch in a simplified simulator: a subset of Arduino C++ translated to JavaScript. Integer division is not truncated, and arrays and Strings are not supported. Not a replacement for real hardware testing.
